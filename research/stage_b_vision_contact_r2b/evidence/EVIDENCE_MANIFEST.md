# R2-B evidence manifest

Raw Isaac RGB-D, physical traces and contact logs remain in the local evidence worktrees and are intentionally ignored from Git because the evidence is large. They were not deleted or rewritten. Each represented run retains compact `summary.json`, camera metadata/timeline, visual qualification, executor/runtime logs and trace summaries in this branch; the older physical04 raw capture remains in the preceding validation worktree. Compact representative images are committed under `selected/`.

Runs: `r2b_rear_wall_sweep01..04`, `r2b_rear_wall_physical01..07`. `physical01` is a runtime-blocked attempt; `physical02` lacks the marker attachment; `physical03` has a camera-init extrinsic mismatch; `physical04` is the earlier valid partial observation episode; `physical05` is the calibration/phase-audit episode; `physical06` is a scheduler-repair probe that exposed asynchronous writer misses; `physical07` is the bounded post-repair episode. The raw physical05/06/07 RGB-D and contact traces remain local and are paired with compact summaries, executor phase receipts, timing metadata, calibration results and representative images.

The corrected phase receipts use executor event intervals rather than Cube ground-truth labels. `physical05` completed release/retreat, regrasp, all 16 insertion slices and the deep-wall seat, then stopped at side-compaction preflight because of the initial `right_fr3_side_suction <-> task26_cube_1` FCL overlap. This is execution evidence, not a physical recovery or full-task PASS claim.

`physical07` records 1550/1550 RGB-depth pairs, no capture/depth errors, median physics receipt interval 0.1667 s, and maximum receipt gap 0.2333 s. The executor reached insertion/deep-wall and entered side-compaction, but the bounded run ended with the existing lift settle failure; this is observation evidence, not a manipulation or recovery PASS. `calibration_static/` contains SHA-256 recorded static marker frames used for the fixed-focal-length principal-point holdout fit. `selected/` contains compact review images; full RGB/depth and JSONL traces stay ignored because of size.
