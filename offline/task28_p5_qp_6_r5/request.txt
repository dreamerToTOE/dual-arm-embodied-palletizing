## Phase 3 — establish a physically meaningful state contract

Create a NEW, source-identified state qualification scheme.

Separate:

A. Source integrity:
   correct robot identity, DOF names, finite q/dq, monotonic physics clock,
   fresh copied arrays, valid link transforms and no competing command sink.

B. Kinematic consistency:
   observed link8/TCP transforms agree with FK,
   joint positions evolve consistently with independently observed movement,
   and moving-state source behavior is reproducible.

C. Solver semantics:
   explicitly account for the documented possibility that end-of-step
   solver velocity differs from frame-average displacement velocity.

D. Settled-state validity:
   distinguish negligible actual displacement from nonzero internal solver
   velocity and determine whether the resulting state is usable for a
   model-based torque controller.

E. Moving-state validity:
   demonstrate meaningful and reproducible joint/link movement,
   signed response, source timestamps and bounded model residuals.

Do not simply increase the historical 0.01 rad/s tolerance.

Do not silently replace solver dq with numerical differentiation.

If any alternative estimated velocity is proposed for control, label it as
an estimator, characterize filtering delay and uncertainty, and validate it
independently against genuine moving trajectories.

A result may be SOLVER_SEMANTICS_EXPLAINED but still CONTROL_SOURCE_BLOCKED.

A result may qualify a bounded controller use without proving every internal
PhysX solver quantity is physically measured.
