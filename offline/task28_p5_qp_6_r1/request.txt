## Phase 2 — compare three dynamics modeling strategies

Study and independently test:

### Strategy F — full free 18-coordinate dynamics

Retain the original full nine-coordinate source test as the negative baseline.

Do not change its historical acceptance threshold or reclassify its failure.

### Strategy K — full dynamics with explicit finger constraints

Represent valid mimic coupling and active finger-limit constraints, where supported.

Use equations conceptually equivalent to:

    M*qdd + h = tau + J_constraint.T*lambda

    J_constraint*qdd + Jdot_constraint*dq = 0

Treat joint-limit constraints as unilateral when applicable, not permanently active bilateral equalities.

Validate constraint identities, feasible motion directions, force units, signs and complementarity assumptions.

Do not invent a constraint reaction sensor.

### Strategy R — verified active-arm reduced dynamics

Investigate a 7-DOF-per-arm control subsystem, while retaining the actual full nine-DOF articulation state and independently defined finger actuation/constraint policy.

Explicitly derive which finger coordinates are held, coupled or treated as constrained.

Do not just slice M[:7,:7] and assume the reduction is valid.

Account for arm/finger inertial coupling and verify when a reduced arm equation is physically justified.

Continue to send full, valid, source-identified effort commands to the Isaac articulations.

The arm controller may be 14D; that does NOT authorize silently discarding the four finger DOFs from the physical model.

Compare all three strategies using the same recorded source where mathematically applicable.
