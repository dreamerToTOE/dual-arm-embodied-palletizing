## Phase 3 — discriminate physical freeze from stale state

For each diagnostic run, record:

- Full nine-DOF left/right q and dq.
- Actual source dtype, shape and object/buffer identity.
- Independent immediate snapshot copy.
- Simulation time and physics step.
- Actual callback dt.
- Link7 and link8 world transforms.
- Actual L-shaped side_suction_tcp world transforms.
- Reported joint velocities and finite-difference velocities.
- Native drive targets and reported gains.
- Articulation sleep/wake status if the installed API supports reliable querying.
- Stage timeline and physics activity.
- Cube transform and unexpected collisions.
- Actual M/c/g, only when their timestamps are meaningful.

Distinguish:

- Physics really stopped advancing.
- Articulation stopped moving but simulation continued.
- The position getter returned a stale state.
- The velocity getter returned a stale state.
- Both getters returned temporally inconsistent states.
- A buffer alias or delayed serialization corrupted observations.
- A drive/solver constraint caused unusual motion without invalid source data.

Do not claim a unique cause without evidence that rules out the alternatives.

Use an independently checked relationship between joint motion and link pose to corroborate the result.

A published sleep mechanism does not by itself explain persistently nonzero joint velocity.
