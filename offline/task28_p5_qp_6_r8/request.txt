## 5. Design a justified feedback controller

Use a torque-domain gravity-compensated joint impedance controller.

Candidate:

    tau = g(q)
          + Kp (q_ref - q)
          + Kd (dq_ref - dq)

Investigate the effects of TGS end-step velocity semantics.

Use the original source-valid q/dq as recorded data.

Do not silently replace native dq with finite-difference velocity.

The controller should not abruptly introduce an inconsistent position reference or demand a discontinuous effort beyond the declared rate limit.

Analyze discrete-time stability and torque/rate feasibility before testing.
