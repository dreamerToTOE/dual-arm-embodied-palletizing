## Phase 4 — new diagnostic closed-loop torque controller

The first control objective is stable FREE_HOME holding, not Cube grasping.

Use a bounded gravity-compensated joint-space PD/impedance controller:

    tau_arm = g_arm(q)
              + Kp*(q_hold - q_arm)
              - Kd*dq_arm

Here q_hold is the actual source-validated reference at the declared ownership transfer.

Use appropriate joint-space torque-domain gains, not acceleration-domain computed-torque gains.

Review the installed solver's TGS velocity semantics when selecting damping and interpreting behavior.

Derive the initial gains and admissible control authority using R6 response magnitudes, actual inertia, 60 Hz dt and observed velocity/position precision.

Do not arbitrarily use large gains.

No unbounded tuning or post-formal gain adjustment.

The control policy must remain research-only and be tested under an explicit bounded diagnostic authorization.

Use R6's actual full nine-coordinate effort adapter.

For each effort write:

- Preserve the native finger policy.
- Check original per-joint effort limits and the stricter research cap.
- In particular, do not apply a 45 N.m research cap in place of smaller source limits for joints 5–7.
- Enforce the registered torque-rate limit.
- Check original scene identity and q/dq timestamps.
- Check FCL and actual tool/Cube clearance.
- Verify exclusive arm effort mode and zero competing position-drive gains.
- Save the proposed command before execution.
- Read back the command.
- Advance exactly one registered physics step.
- Validate the resulting state before continuing.

Do not treat FREE_HOME as an attached bilateral grasp.
